\paragraph{Replication across splits.} Four earlier, non-confirmatory splits of the same protocol family
(development and gate of v1b and v2, on the standard template) show
which conclusions depend on wording (Figure~\ref{fig:replication}). $D_{\mathrm{S}}$ was
positive in all ten split-by-task estimates (3.133 to 6.599 nats), and $\Delta_{\mathrm{SO}}$ in
nine of ten. $\Delta_{\mathrm{SM}}$ never favored the superseded assignment. On the standard template,
however, it ranged from $-0.806$ to $+0.163$, with six of eight intervals including zero. The size of the
mention advantage depends on wording.

\paragraph{H3 method.} Each fold's model is a pipeline of feature standardization (zero mean, unit
variance) followed by an L2-regularized logistic regression ($C=1$, lbfgs, no tuning). Both steps are fitted
on the training folds only, so the held-out fold never informs the scaling. Folds are grouped by history
(five folds). For each history we average, over its items, the out-of-fold log loss without minus with
$R_{\mathrm{easy}}$; the reported gain and its 95\% interval come from the history bootstrap of these
per-history differences (2{,}000 draws, fixed seed).

\paragraph{Qwen3-14B details.} Its frozen gate passed in all three routing cells and in no access cell. On the
confirmation, routing retrieval was 0.989--0.997 for the current state and 0.997 for the initial state, and
current-only decisions were 0.977--0.992 correct. In routing, the net switch rate is $0.167$ over all
histories (67 switched to wrong, 3 to correct) and $0.165$ $[0.128, 0.205]$ in the 376 tracked histories. In
generated switches the attribute difference is not resolved ($0.167$ against $0.146$, paired difference
\swattrfourteen). The mention and the superseded assignment had similar switch rates: $0.169$ against
$0.167$, paired difference (mention $-$ superseded) \swmentionfourteen, with $\Delta_{\mathrm{SM}} = +0.248$
$[-0.117, 0.601]$; on the histories tracked under all four constructions (Table~\ref{tab:tracked-models}) the
difference was $-0.003$ $[-0.050, 0.042]$. In access (non-competent cells) the superseded assignment moved
preference more than the mention ($\Delta_{\mathrm{SM}} = +0.845$ $[0.425, 1.255]$). Easy-query sensitivity did not improve prediction: AUROC $0.684$ without and $0.685$ with
$R_{\mathrm{easy}}$, log-loss gain $-0.0001$ $[-0.0027, 0.0022]$.
