# Manuscript results and reproducibility index

The manuscript's core evidence is relational/order Experiment 1 (96 histories, Qwen3-8B and Gemma 3 4B) and marker × construction Experiment 2 (96 histories, same models; an earlier 24-history run is reported in Appendix D). It also reports three controlled follow-ups, five additional screened models (seven total), an exploratory OLMo-2 generated-answer analysis, and distractor development that stopped under its rule. The update-chain generation development remained at ceiling; a separate 96-history score-only analysis is reported. The earlier `cross_model_v1` result is historical context in Appendix A and has a different estimand.

`paper/figures/make_figures.py` rebuilds Figures 2 and 3 and the Appendix B order figure from the per-history CSVs with the manuscript's 2,000-draw, seed-73021 percentile history bootstrap; `scripts/fig_decomposition.py` rebuilds Figure 1 from the sealed analyses. `paper/data/export_history_estimates.py` re-exports the CSVs from the sealed analysis JSONs when the local `outputs/` artifacts are available. The CSVs are compact derived estimates, not raw prompt scores: they reproduce the displayed means and intervals, but not token scoring from raw prompts.

Raw datasets, score files, and sealed analyses are excluded from version control, and some source artifacts referenced by export scripts are not present in this checkout. The tracked `paper/data/` tables and JSON summaries reproduce the manuscript's displayed means and bootstrap summaries; independent rescoring or full reanalysis requires access to the corresponding raw artifacts. Protocol records document hashes where those were available.
