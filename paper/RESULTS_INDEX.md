# Manuscript results and reproducibility index

The manuscript's primary evidence is relational/order Experiment 1 (96 histories, Qwen3-8B and Gemma 3 4B) and the marker × construction Experiment 2 (96 histories, same models; an earlier 24-history run is reported in Appendix D). Follow-ups: the name–value distance study (96 histories), the update-chain study (development only; stopped by its rule), additional models on Experiment 1, and the distractor study (development only; stopped by its rule). The earlier `cross_model_v1` result is historical context in Appendix A and has a different estimand.

`paper/figures/make_figures.py` rebuilds Figures 2 and 3 and the Appendix B order figure from the per-history CSVs with the manuscript's 2,000-draw, seed-73021 percentile history bootstrap; `scripts/fig_decomposition.py` rebuilds Figure 1 from the sealed analyses. `paper/data/export_history_estimates.py` re-exports the CSVs from the sealed analysis JSONs when the local `outputs/` artifacts are available. The CSVs are compact derived estimates, not raw prompt scores: they reproduce the displayed means and intervals, but not token scoring from raw prompts.

The source datasets, score files, and sealed JSON analyses are in the working checkout under `outputs/` but are excluded from version control; the protocol records document their hashes and validation. A clean Git checkout reproduces the figures and bootstrap summaries from `paper/data/`, but not the scores themselves without the archived outputs.
