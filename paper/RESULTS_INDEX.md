# Manuscript results and reproducibility index

The manuscript's primary evidence is relational/order Experiment 1 (96 histories, Qwen3-8B and Gemma 3 4B) and the separately specified marker confirmation Experiment 2 (24 histories, same models). The harder-task distractor study is development-only: its prespecified stopping rule prevented test generation. The earlier `cross_model_v1` result is presented as historical context in Appendix A and has a different estimand.

`paper/figures/make_figures.py` rebuilds Figures 1 and 2 from the per-history CSVs using the manuscript's 2,000-draw, seed-73021 percentile history bootstrap. `paper/data/export_history_estimates.py` re-exports these CSVs from the sealed analysis JSONs when those local `outputs/` artifacts are available. The CSVs are compact derived estimates, not raw prompt scores; they let readers reproduce the displayed means and history-bootstrap intervals, including the paired marker construction gaps, but they do not independently recompute token scoring or the full analyses from raw prompts.

The source datasets, score files, and sealed JSON analyses are present in the working checkout under `outputs/` but are excluded from version control. The result ledger documents their hashes and validation. A reader with only a clean Git checkout can reproduce the figures and bootstrap summaries from `paper/data/`, but cannot independently recompute them from raw scores without the corresponding archived outputs.
